More of the model shelf

Built for one shared battlefield.

The model families include the procedural infantry tiers; authored cavalry and horse; Gribeauval and Olive artillery; caissons, carts, and wagons; 22 battlefield village buildings; twenty-two town-edge building placements from fourteen reusable models with floor plans and furnished room close-ups; 55 cataloged town, camp, farm, and battlefield props with embedded PBR materials; oak and ground cover; and reusable fieldwork and player-item kits.

Source and runtime

Renders show asset appearance.

Most cards are Blender source renders. Some base models and village props adapt licensed source work, while the project’s armor, weapons, artillery, terrain, and procedural kits include original Big Sim models. Runtime behavior and performance remain tied to each asset’s Unity review.

Credits

Share the source with the image.

The combined Winged Hussar uses adapted head and hand geometry by Andy Woodhead and a horse model by abhayexe. Battlefield portraits also show adapted models by Andy Woodhead, perceval-66, and Timothy Hsu. Village and camp source dressing includes work by Booba Booba, Fri13Day, and Nicholas-3D.
